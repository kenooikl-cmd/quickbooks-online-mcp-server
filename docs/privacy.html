Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

AMS MCP is a private integration used by authorized personnel to access their connected QuickBooks Online company data.

Data accessed

The integration may access accounting records, customers, suppliers, transactions, reports, and company information that an authorized QuickBooks administrator permits through Intuit OAuth.

How data is used

Data is used only to answer authorized business queries and perform actions explicitly requested by an authorized user. The integration does not sell QuickBooks data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

The MCP server runs locally. OAuth credentials and any locally generated output remain under the operator's control. Access should be limited to authorized users, and write or delete capabilities should remain disabled unless specifically required.

Sharing and retention

QuickBooks data is not intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties. Local logs or generated files are retained only as needed for internal business use and may be deleted by the operator.

Your choices

A QuickBooks administrator can revoke the app's access from the QuickBooks account. Revocation prevents future API access but does not automatically remove files previously saved locally.
